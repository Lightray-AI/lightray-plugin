# LightRay

Ask Claude to brief you on the companies in your next meetings, or to take a first look at a new client. With a calendar connected in Claude, the brief reads your meetings and the organizations of the people in them; otherwise, name the companies. For each company you pick, LightRay's report gives the background, any questions to ask and what to do next, with a link to the full report.

Each company new to you uses one of your LightRay reports, and Claude asks before it starts one. Reading a report or listing yours uses none.

## What to ask

- Brief me on tomorrow's meetings
- Brief me on the companies I meet this week
- First look at *Company name, City*, a new client

## Add it to Claude

1. In Claude, open Customize, then Plugins, and look for LightRay on the Discover tab. If it isn't there, select Add, then Add marketplace, choose Add from a repository, and enter Lightray-AI/lightray-plugin.
2. Select LightRay, then Add.
3. On the plugin's Connectors tab, connect LightRay and sign in with your email. LightRay emails you a link and a six-digit code. There is no password.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner adds LightRay for the organization first. Each person then connects it with their own email.

## Your free reports

Each email address you sign in with has three free reports. Checking the same company again doesn't count, and neither does a report that fails on LightRay's side. After your three free reports, LightRay quotes a price for more, for you or your firm. Write to hello@lightray.ai.

## What it runs, sends and fetches

- The plugin is two skills, meeting-brief and first-look, and the address of LightRay's connector, https://app.lightray.ai/mcp. It runs no code of its own and stores nothing.
- Claude sends LightRay the names of the companies you pick, with their city or country, and fetches your LightRay reports and their links. Nothing else from your meetings goes to LightRay: no names of people, email addresses or meeting details.
- For a meeting brief, Claude reads your meetings through a calendar you have connected in Claude, and only when you ask.
- You sign in to LightRay with your email address. LightRay keeps it as its privacy notice says: https://www.lightray.ai/v3/privacy. By using LightRay you accept its terms of use: https://www.lightray.ai/v3/terms.
